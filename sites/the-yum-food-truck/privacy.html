Privacy Policy

Last updated: effective date

Draft for review. This page is a starting draft and needs review by a qualified lawyer before the site goes live.

This policy explains what personal information The Yum Food Truck collects through this website and how we use it.

What we collect

When you send a catering request, we receive the details you enter: your name, phone number, email address if you give it, and your event date, guest count, location and notes.

We do not use advertising trackers on this site. Update this if you add analytics, such as Google Analytics.

Maps and links to other services

Our home page shows an embedded Google Map so you can find the truck. When the map loads, Google may collect information such as your IP address under its own privacy policy. Links to Google Maps and Google reviews take you to Google's site.

How the catering form works

When you press Send request, your details are placed into a text message in your phone's messaging app, addressed to (954) 557-3972. Nothing is sent until you send that message yourself.

How we use your information

  • To reply to your request and arrange your booking.
  • To contact you about that booking, such as changes on the day.

We do not sell your information or use it for marketing without your permission.

Who we share it with

We share your details only with people who help us deliver your booking, or when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep booking details for as long as we need them to deliver your event and keep normal business records, then delete them. State your retention period.

Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the information we hold about you. Call or text (954) 557-3972.

Children

This site is not aimed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top of this page shows when it last changed.
