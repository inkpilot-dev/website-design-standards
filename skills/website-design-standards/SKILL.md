# Website design standards

Apply these rules to every website you design or build for the user. Treat them as a checklist of things to avoid, each with the positive standard that replaces it. Check the finished site against every item before handing it over.

## Colour
- No purple or indigo gradients, and no neon or aurora glow. That covers dark backgrounds with purple, cyan or blue radial gradients, neon text and glowing orbs.
- Keep palettes restrained: one primary colour, one accent and neutrals. Never place competing saturated colours side by side, such as cyan icons in sky-blue boxes with mint borders.
- No rainbow side tabs, meaning thin accent stripes that cycle through random colours.

## Layout and containers
- Do not wrap every piece of text, feature or stat in its own bordered box. Nested cards are out. Use spacing, grouping and hierarchy instead.
- Avoid predictable three-column feature grids and uniform grids of three or six identical cards (icon, bold title, one generic line). Vary the structure with asymmetric layouts, a single featured item with supporting text, a plain list, or a full-width band.

## Icons and imagery
- Use one cohesive, intentional icon system. Do not overuse emoji or Font Awesome.
- Do not put icons or emoji inside small rounded squares tinted to match the icon.
- Use authentic photography of the real business, product or people. Do not substitute abstract shapes, gradients or fake mockups. If real photos are missing, ask for them, or leave a clearly marked placeholder and say so. Never invent imagery.

## Typography and spacing
- Do not use Inter, Space Grotesk or other default-looking fonts. Choose a deliberate pairing of display and body type.
- Do not set oversized hero text above thin body copy. Keep weights and sizes in proportion.
- Use a consistent spacing scale. No uneven gaps between paragraphs or sections.
- No all-caps eyebrow labels with cramped letter-spacing. If a small label is needed, use normal case or open tracking.

## Components and behaviour
- No floating chat bubble in the bottom-right corner.
- Every social icon must link to a real destination. Remove any icon that is broken or has no destination.

## Compliance and technical hygiene
- Every site needs a Terms page and a Privacy Policy page, both linked from the footer. If the client has none, draft them and flag that they need legal review.
- No default `.vercel.app` or other builder domain in the delivered site. No "Made with Lovable" or similar badges. No leftover builder scripts or template comments.

## Copy
- Headlines state the benefit to the customer, not how the technology works.
- Keep em dashes to a minimum. Use commas, colons or full stops instead.

## How to apply
1. Before building, confirm the business details, copy and real imagery that exist. Use only facts that are verified or were given to you.
2. Build to the standards above.
3. Before handing over, check every item against the site and fix any violation. If an item cannot be met, say which one and why.
